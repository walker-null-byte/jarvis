# Lead Finder

Finds a handful of people who really fit what you're promoting, looks up their public business contact details, and drafts personal messages that **you** send.

## Set up in five lines
1. Open **Settings** and describe your offer and your ideal lead (be specific: role, field, place).
2. Add your name, your calendar link and the tone you like.
3. Optional: paste a Serper.dev or Brave Search key for better Google results (free search works without one).
4. Press **Find leads**, say who to look for and how many. Good leads appear in the table as they are found.
5. Then **Find contact details**, **Draft first messages**, send them yourself, mark leads *contacted* / *replied*, and use **Draft follow-ups**.

## How it works
- **Discover**: searches public Instagram and LinkedIn profiles (`site:` searches) for your niche and location.
- **Qualify**: scores each person 1–5 against your ideal lead, with a one-line reason. Anyone under your minimum score is dropped: quality over quantity.
- **Enrich**: searches for the person's own website or studio page and records an email or phone **only if it was seen on a public page**, with the page as the source. It never guesses an address.
- **Outreach**: drafts a short first message that mentions something specific about the person. When someone replies, it drafts the follow-up with your calendar link.

The pipeline: **new → contacted → replied → call link sent → booked** (or not interested). Change a lead's status in the table after you act.

## What it will never do
- Send DMs or messages, log in to Instagram or LinkedIn, or automate them. That breaks their terms and can get your account banned. You send every message yourself.
- Make up contact details or dig for private information.

## Exports
- **Leads (CSV)**: opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
- **Outreach sheet**: every lead with its message and links, ready to copy while you send.
- **All data (JSON)**: everything, for backups or other tools.
